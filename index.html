What the DPDP Act asks of your business

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies to organisations that process digital personal data. As a Data Fiduciary, these are the core obligations we help you meet.

  • Clear noticeTell people what data you collect, why, and how to exercise their rights.
  • Valid consentFree, specific and informed consent that is as easy to withdraw as to give.
  • Purpose and retention limitsUse data only for the stated purpose and erase it when no longer needed.
  • Security safeguardsReasonable security measures to prevent personal data breaches.
  • Breach notificationInform the Data Protection Board and each affected person when a breach occurs.
  • Data Principal rightsAccess, correction, erasure, grievance redressal and nomination.
  • Children’s dataVerifiable parental consent, and no tracking or targeted advertising directed at children.
  • Processor accountabilityYou remain responsible for data your vendors process on your behalf.
  • Significant Data FiduciariesAdditional duties, including a Data Protection Officer, independent audits and impact assessments.

Penalties under the Act reach up to ₹250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards. Already ISO 27001 certified? Much of your security work carries over.

Frequently asked questions

How long does ISO 27001 certification take?

For most small and mid-sized organisations, it takes several months to a year from gap assessment to certificate. It depends on your scope, how mature your current controls are, and how much time your team can give the project. After a gap assessment we give you a realistic timeline.

What is the difference between ISO 27001 and ISO 27002?

ISO/IEC 27001 sets out the requirements for an information security management system and is the standard you are certified against. ISO/IEC 27002 is guidance on how to implement the security controls listed in ISO 27001 Annex A. You cannot be certified to ISO 27002.

Does the DPDP Act apply to my business?

If you process digital personal data in India, or process it outside India in connection with offering goods or services to people in India, the Act very likely applies to you, whatever your size. A short assessment confirms your role and obligations.

Do you issue the ISO certificate?

No. Certificates are issued by independent, accredited certification bodies. Consultants should never certify the systems they helped build. We prepare you for the audit, help you choose a certification body and support you through the Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits.

Can ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 be implemented together?

Yes. Both follow the same harmonised management system structure, so policies, risk management, internal audit and management review can be shared. Doing them together saves time and avoids duplicate documentation.
