Common questions

How long does ISO 27001 certification take?

It depends on your size, scope and starting point. Many organisations take several months from gap assessment to certification audit. After a short assessment we give you a realistic timeline for your situation.

Does the DPDPA apply to my business?

If you process digital personal data of individuals in India, it very likely does, whatever your size. We can confirm your obligations and what to prioritise first.

Do you issue the ISO certificate?

No. Certificates are issued by independent accredited certification bodies. We prepare you for the audit, help you choose a certification body and support you through the audit itself.

Can you combine ISO 27001 and ISO 42001?

Yes. Both follow the same management system structure, so we can build them together and avoid duplicated work.

What does the free assessment include?

A short call to understand your goals, followed by a summary of where you stand and the steps we recommend. There is no obligation to continue.
