Frequently asked questions

How long does ISO 27001 certification take?

For most small and mid-sized organisations, several months to a year from gap assessment to certificate, depending on scope, how mature your current controls are and how much time your team can give the project.

Does the DPDP Act apply to my business?

If you process digital personal data of customers, employees or users in India, it very likely does, whatever your size. A short assessment confirms your obligations and what to prioritise.

How do I know if my business needs an ERP?

Common signs are running finance, inventory or sales across disconnected spreadsheets and tools, re-entering the same data in several places, slow month-end closing, and leaders waiting days for reliable reports.

How long does an ERP implementation take?

It depends on the system, the number of modules and how much data needs to move. Smaller rollouts can take a few months; larger ones longer. Good requirements and process mapping up front is what keeps projects on schedule.

What does an operational efficiency review involve?

We map how work actually flows through your teams, measure where time and cost are lost, and give you a prioritised list of improvements, from quick wins to larger process or system changes.

Can you help with compliance and transformation at the same time?

Yes, and it is usually cheaper that way. Building controls into new systems and processes from the start avoids reworking them later for an audit.
